What the numbers are. Sim Score is the average of 20,000 Monte Carlo simulations of this matchup. Win % is how often the named team wins those simulations outright. Cover % is how often the model's preferred side covers the listed market number (spread) or lands on the listed total (O/U), pushes excluded — the model's own estimate, measured against the real line shown.

LOCKED vs preview. A LOCKED row is written to the permanent record and gets graded against the closing line after the game; a preview row locks at a later update nearer kickoff (within 3 hours), once the lines and lineups are final. The Season Results tab keeps the running scorecard of every locked pick — that graded record, not these percentages, is the proof of what the model can actually do. For reference, the market's closing line has historically been the more accurate predictor (margin RMSE 15.82 vs the market's ~15.1 on ~1,800 graded games), and 52.38% is the win rate needed to profit at standard −110 pricing.

Columns. Sim Score is the mean simulated score, away – home; its total is shrunk toward the league mean — unshrunk totals scored worse than guessing the average. Market Line is quoted from the home side, negative = home favoured. Cover % is the Monte Carlo probability that the named pick beats the listed market number, excluding pushes.
Method. Ridge-regularised opponent-adjusted offence/defence ratings (λ and recency decay chosen by walk-forward cross-validation), simulated 20,000 times per game with a drive-based multinomial that reproduces real key-number clustering on margins of 3 and 7. Noise is calibrated from out-of-sample backtest residuals. Ratings correlate +0.99 with ESPN FPI.
Not included in the line. Pass-vs-pass and rush-vs-rush style matchup was tested and had no predictive signal (t = −0.39), so it never moves these numbers.
