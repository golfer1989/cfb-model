What the numbers are. Sim Score is the average of 20,000 Monte Carlo simulations of this matchup. Win % is how often the named team wins those simulations outright. Cover % is how often the model's preferred side covers the listed market number (spread) or lands on the listed total (O/U), pushes excluded — the model's own estimate, measured against the real line shown.

LOCKED vs preview. A LOCKED row is written to the permanent record and gets graded against the closing line after the game; a preview row locks at a later update nearer kickoff (within 3 hours), once the lines and lineups are final. The Season Results tab keeps the running scorecard of every locked pick — that graded record, not these percentages, is the proof of what the model can actually do. Columns. Sim Score is the mean simulated score, away – home; its total is shrunk toward the league mean — unshrunk totals scored worse than guessing the average. Method. Ridge-regularised opponent-adjusted offence/defence ratings (λ and recency decay chosen by walk-forward cross-validation), simulated 20,000 times per game with a drive-based multinomial that reproduces real key-number clustering on margins of 3 and 7. Noise is calibrated from out-of-sample backtest residuals. Ratings correlate +0.99 with ESPN FPI.
